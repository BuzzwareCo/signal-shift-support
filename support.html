Signal Shift Support

Help with gameplay, saved progress, audio, Game Center and accessibility.

Quick help

How do I complete a circuit?

Build a continuous powered route from the white lightning bolt to the red target. The white glow shows the route currently carrying power.

How do I avoid losing progress?

Open Pause and choose Save game. The main menu shows whether a saved run can be restored. Restoring or restarting a level uses one retry.

I cannot hear the overload warning

Open Audio and check Sound effects. You can enable Overload warning vibration independently. The board also pulses and shakes as overload approaches 100%.

Game Center is not updating

Confirm that you are signed into Game Center in iOS Settings, then reopen Signal Shift. Assisted, test and practice runs are not submitted to worldwide leaderboards.

How do I replay a tutorial?

Use Practice on the main menu for hands-on lessons. During a run, open the game menu and choose Replay in-game lessons.

Contact support

For a bug report or support request, open a GitHub issue. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Signal Shift version, and a brief description of what happened. Avoid posting personal information.

Before reporting a problem

  1. Update Signal Shift and iOS to the latest available versions.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Check the in-game Quick Guide for the relevant mechanic.
  4. Include a screenshot if it helps explain the issue.
