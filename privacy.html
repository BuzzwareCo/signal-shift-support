Privacy Policy

Signal Shift is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

Information stored on your device

Signal Shift stores gameplay information locally on your device. This can include settings, saved runs, scores, level progress, achievements, earned Sparks, equipment, tutorial progress and retry availability.

Buzzware does not receive this locally stored gameplay information. Removing the app may remove it unless iOS restores it from a device backup.

Apple Game Center

If you use Game Center, Apple provides authentication and leaderboard services. Signal Shift submits eligible scores and associated level information to Game Center. Apple may process your Game Center player identifier, display name and leaderboard activity under Apple’s own privacy policy.

You can manage Game Center through iOS Settings. Signal Shift does not receive your Apple Account password.

Information we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data
  • No payment details or real-money purchases
  • No Signal Shift account or profile

Children’s privacy

Signal Shift does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Game Center availability and family controls are managed by Apple and the device owner.

Support requests

If you voluntarily create a support request on GitHub, GitHub processes the information you provide under its own privacy policy. Support requests may be public, so please do not include private or sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Signal Shift’s features or data practices change. The latest version and its revision date will remain available on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Signal Shift support repository.
